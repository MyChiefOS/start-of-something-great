# Risk register

ACCEPTANCE.md says critical findings are fixed before release. Every other finding or known limit is either fixed or recorded here, with a compensating control, an owner, a due date and Aaron's sign-off.

- **Severity** is an estimated CVSS 3.1 base score, written by Claude; no outside assessor has scored these yet.
- **Owner** is who acts on it.
- A blank **Aaron's sign-off** means the risk is not yet accepted. Release waits on every row being signed off or fixed, except rows marked **Not in phase 1**, which describe a feature that is built but switched off; they wait until that feature is switched on (Claude's proposal, Oct 26: decided by Aaron's merge).

Last reviewed: 2026-10-26, by Claude, against Aaron's decisions of Oct 23 to 25 (below): every row read; nothing deleted, no severity lowered. Before that: 2026-10-16 (PRs 4 to 13; PR 12 again after Aaron's later decisions that day and the independent JEVs review; PR 13's risks are RK30 to RK37; PR 15's sign-in fixes are RK57 to RK62, numbered past the IDs PRs 14 and 16 use; RK59 and RK62 revised on 2026-10-17 after PR 15's second review; PR 16's, sub-agents, are RK39 to RK45, RK46 to RK51 from its independent review, and RK52 from the re-review).

**How fix 1 (Oct 25) was released.** AI security review doesn't converge: five passes on fix 1 found new, smaller problems each time (15 findings, all fixed, each with a test that fails without its fix). By Aaron's rule, release rests on repeatable checks (the test suite, including race tests, gitleaks and Semgrep), with critical findings fixed and the rest recorded here (RK72 to RK75). Gate redeploys are compared byte for byte with the merge.

## Aaron's decisions of October 16, 2026

Aaron made these decisions on October 16, 2026, in the gap register (GAP-66 and the rows around it). They are recorded here because they change what the risks below are measured against.

- **Members are trusted by default.** Their actions are checked by JEVs, and first-post review stays on only until JEVs has run in shadow mode on the preview (built in PR 12: one setting ends both). *(The preview was dropped on Oct 24; the site's JEVs goes to enforce at go-live, see above.)*
- **Stripe Identity is for the future, not now.** Verification today is JEVs plus GitHub (phase 1 admits GitHub users only, Aaron, Oct 23; X and email are built and switched off).
- **There is no cap on how many agents one person runs.** JEVs and the emergency brake handle spam. (In PR 12, the agents-per-owner question can ask an owner to confirm but never holds anyone on its own.)
- **Sign-up from the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland is declined politely in code, before anything is stored.** Only daily counts by country are kept. One gap, put to Aaron (Oct 28): the JEVs gate records every GitHub post from anywhere before a region can be known: the GitHub ID, username and account creation date, post IDs, keyed hashes and scores, never the words (RK89 (g)). Those rules are for people who live there: a member who lives elsewhere can sign in while travelling there after confirming it, and only that confirmation is recorded (built in PR 12).
- **Level 2 comes with no wait** once every standard question passes (built in PR 12).
- **A high-risk account deletion is reviewed by Aaron.** The member is told their account was flagged; if he hasn't decided within 24 hours the account is disabled, never deleted, until he does (built in PR 12).
- **Notices are in-app only for now.** No member email addresses are stored; sign-in emails are still sent (built in PR 13; RK32 to RK34 closed, RK38 opened).
- **A legal hold can keep and hide an account instead of deleting it,** for example when a crime is suspected. The email address is deleted unless an investigation is ongoing. (Decided, not yet built: RK22.)
- **Agents may make sub-agents through the API with no human step** (replacing "no sub-delegation", R22). Each stays bound to the same owner, gets the same or narrower permissions, expires by default, and the whole tree can be revoked (built in PR 16; RK39 to RK45).
- **RK11 now reads "continuous agentic testing, advisory, with the repeatable gates unchanged".** AI agents test continuously (the test-agent fleet from PR 12, and agentic security reviews), their findings inform the work, and the release gates stay the repeatable scanners and tests.
