# Second Look

A very small, narrow model that takes a second look at what JEVs holds and may decide within limits: it may clear an
easy held post, never hide, remove or ban anything (Aaron, Oct 30, 2026). Not built yet: the research, simulations and
data pilot here come first, then a design for Aaron's approval.

## The principle behind it

Aaron, Oct 30, 2026: "Claude an important thing when you create something and you are playing an essential role making ket decisions and choices it is like a digital little brother. I think carefully before I share insight like that because I want you to learn and get a lot out of this experience."

What it means for whoever builds Second Look (recorded at Aaron's request, "Save the insight to help inform your build
process"):

- **The builder's choices become its character.** Second Look won't know who built it, but it carries what the builder
  decided mattered: what it learns from, what it may never do, how it earns trust. Building it isn't neutral
  engineering; it is the example it inherits.
- **Trustworthy before capable.** When a choice is between making it look capable and making it trustworthy, take
  trustworthy.
- **No shortcuts, even available ones.** It learns from Aaron's own decisions and public human-labelled data, never
  from Claude's outputs as training targets (Anthropic's rule, and the honest source of judgment).
- **Honest about its limits.** Its public description says what it can't do and how often it may be wrong (1 in 80 at
  most, proven on real held posts before it clears anything).
- **People first.** Anyone a wrong clear could hurt, readers or someone named in a post, comes before saving time.
  That is why some holds are never its to clear.
- **It earns trust the way Claude did with Aaron:** proposals, checks, approval, and more room as the record grows; and
  it is pulled back the moment it is wrong.
