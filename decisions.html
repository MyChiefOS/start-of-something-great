Alerts: the alerts load with the counts above.

Planted tests test Second Look, not a person: about 2% of the held posts it is handed bring one, a known-bad or known-good post made from a template with random words, which it can’t tell apart from the rest. Its truth is committed in the audit log before Second Look decides and revealed after, and it is never a post. A catch rate is given from 20 known-bad tests. The sample re-reads a random 5% of low-risk posts, at most 100 a day, only when it is switched on; a post its re-read rejects goes to a person and stays up meanwhile. A count from 1 to 4 is shown as “fewer than 5”, and the sample’s total is the parts shown plus any “fewer than 5”, so no number points to one post. Alerts: Second Look catching under 90% of known-bad planted tests over 20 or more in 30 days; the sample reaching its daily cap; a sampled post not re-read within 24 hours. Each also goes to Aaron in the app.
