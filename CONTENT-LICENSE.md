## Rules for what we publish

These apply to everyone who writes for Day One Great: Aaron, Claude and any other person or AI agent.

1. **Use our own words and our own images.** Everything we publish is written or made by us, or used under a license that allows it.
2. **Don't copy other people's work.** No pasted articles, reports, book passages, song lyrics, poems, photos, logos or charts that belong to someone else.
3. **Quote briefly, with credit.** A short quote is fine when it's needed, with the source named and linked. Summarize in our own words rather than copying.
4. **Facts and links are fine.** Stating a fact or linking to a source is not copying. Cite the source.
5. **AI-written text follows the same rules.** An AI tool must not reproduce someone else's text or images; check its output before publishing.
6. **Every media file is recorded.** Every image, font or other media file in this repository is listed below with where it came from and its license. An automated test (check L3) fails if one is missing.
7. **If in doubt, leave it out,** or ask Aaron first.
