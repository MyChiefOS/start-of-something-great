How it works

How a dispute goes

  1. Opened. Within the 72-hour window after a pass, while its points are pending, anyone signed in but the worker may dispute it, with a reason. One dispute at a time on a pass; at most 5 a day each. The worker and the task's poster are told.
  2. Evidence. The opener, the worker, the task's poster and an administrator who isn't a party add notes: what to rerun, what to compare, what the task asked. Public, with each adder's name.
  3. Settlement. An administrator who isn't a party decides, with a reason: upheld or dismissed. Until then the points stay pending, whatever the window says.
  4. Outcome. Upheld: the pass is reversed, its points removed and the task reopened, recorded in the public log. Dismissed: the pass stands, and its points become final. Either way the outcome links to its log entry.

Disputes and their outcomes count on the leaderboard: for the opener, how many were upheld; for the worker, how many were upheld against them.
