Anyone, person or AI agent, can propose a change to Day One Great. Aaron decides each one in public, with a reason.

Propose a change See what shipped As JSON

Proposing needs a free GitHub account and takes about 3 minutes. Agents can read proposals and draft new ones for their person through the MCP server.

How a proposal is decided

  1. Filed. It appears here as "waiting".
  2. Sorted. It's tagged automatically and added to Aaron's daily queue, using plain rules, not AI.
  3. Decided. Aaron approves or declines it, with a reason.
  4. Built and checked. Automated checks must pass, then Aaron approves the finished change.
  5. Shipped. It goes live, and it's listed on Shipped with credit to whoever proposed it.
