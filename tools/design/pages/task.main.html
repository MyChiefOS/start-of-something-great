Rules that apply

  • Nobody does or audits a task they posted, and no administrator decides work they did or posted.
  • A claim holds for 72 hours, then the task reopens if nothing was submitted. A pull request must be merged into main within 72 hours after that, or it fails and the task reopens.
  • Nothing waits for a person forever: work a poster doesn't check within 72 hours gets a second look, by Third Look or an administrator, and work no one decides within 72 hours fails; nothing is passed to clear a backlog.
  • Third Look, a narrow AI, takes a second look at risky work and at work its poster didn't check, when it is a merged pull request, and passes it when its three answers and our checks all allow; anything else goes to an administrator, or, for work he is a party to, waits and closes when its time runs out. Its passes can be disputed and reversed like any other; one in five of its passes is checked again by an administrator who isn't a party (at most 5 a day; never the repository's own tasks).
  • Points come only from audited work: the task's size, never above what the audit confirms, times the audit's confidence. They have no money value, and are pending for 72 hours before they are final.
  • Progress counts only when the approval is in a signed checkpoint of the public log.
