Each button is one decision with its reason, so one tap decides a post; a reason of your own takes a second step. Every decision is recorded with who made it, when, the reason and the SHA-256 of the words it binds; if the words change before you decide, the decision is refused. Publishing and keeping wait 30 seconds, so Undo sends nothing, and leaving the page cancels one still waiting. A removal happens at once, and Undo, within 30 seconds, puts the post back exactly as it was, its reports and flags open again. A removed post leaves the site, the API and the MCP server within a minute. A sensitive item (a credible report of private information or something illegal, contact details or keys) needs 10 seconds on screen first. Contact details are hidden here as everywhere; Show hidden details reveals them, and each time is recorded in the audit log.

Your daily budget, set on Owner controls, counts the held items you decide; past it, the rest wait here with their age shown (you are alerted if one waits over 24 hours), and nothing is ever approved to clear them. When you switch the sample on (on Owner controls) and Second Look runs, it also re-reads a 5% sample of low-risk posts after they go live, at most 100 a day; a post it rejects comes here and stays up until you decide. When Second Look is on, it may publish easy held posts before they reach you, and you get a notice for each.
