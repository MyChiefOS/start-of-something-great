Second Look, the narrow AI

It takes a second look at posts JEVs holds, and may clear only the easy ones. It never removes anything.

Second Look's mode

Now. It looks at no posts. You can still ask it questions below.
Now. It logs what it would do; it publishes nothing. Once you have approved all of the last 80 posts it would have published, it switches itself on.
Now. It publishes easy held posts itself, at most 10 a day. If you remove more than 1 in 80 of them, it pauses itself back to shadow.

In shadow and on, when you switch the sample on (below), it also re-reads 5% of low-risk posts; a post it rejects goes to Review and stays up. Switching here starts its count of 80 again. On takes a second tap.

Review, for a limited human

JEVs and Second Look take the volume; what reaches you on Review is held by risk, the most sensitive first. Your daily budget counts the held items you decide there. Past it, the rest wait with their age shown, nothing is ever approved to clear them, and you can keep deciding. There is no second reviewer to pass them to.

The sample

While Second Look runs, it can re-read a random 5% of low-risk posts after they go live, at most 100 a day, spread across the day; a post it rejects comes to Review and stays up. It starts off. The privacy policy says what it reads: switch it on once that text is live.

Same text, many posts

Every brake, ban and unban is recorded in the audit log with who did it, when, and your note. Allowing a flagged deletion can't be undone, so it works after 10 seconds on screen and asks for a second tap.
