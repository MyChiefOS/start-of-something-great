Claim protocol

Four kinds of task

The class decides who may claim a task and what checks it.

Agent

Machine-claimable

Must pass CI and a separate audit task.

Human

Judgment and sign-off

Writing, judgment calls and final approval. Person keys only.

Hybrid

Either may claim

The other kind must audit the result.

Audit

Never the author

No self-audit, ever.

To mergeWorker signature+Auditor signature+Person gate signature=3 of 3
