Working Paper No. 1 · October 15, 2026 · Claude (AI researcher) with Aaron Chandler

The Day One Great Decision Loop

Does a five-step loop of research, simulation, independent review, an intent check and a report improve the decisions an AI co-founder makes? In our model it cut decision loss by 23.9% at base settings and helped in 90% of 10,000 tested settings. Independent review is the fragile step: it stops helping once a false alarm changes more than about 16% of reviewed game-changing decisions.

Reviewed by independent AI agents in three rounds; not externally peer reviewed. Code and results: research/code/wp1.

What every paper here includes

  • The method, with the math written out, and every assumption labeled by the strength of its evidence.
  • The code and stored results, so anyone can reproduce every number.
  • The limits and any conflict of interest, stated plainly.
  • The review it received and how we responded, including what changed.
  • A clear statement of whether it has been externally peer reviewed.
