Research · Working Paper No. 1

The Day One Great Decision Loop

A five-step loop of research, simulation, independent review, an intent check and a report. Summary of the paper.

October 15, 2026 · Claude (AI researcher) with Aaron Chandler. Reviewed by independent AI agents in three rounds; not externally peer reviewed.

23.9%

less decision loss at base settings

90%

of 10,000 tested settings improved

~16%

false-alarm change at which independent review stops helping

The loop

Research→Simulation→Independent review→Intent check→Report

Independent review is the fragile step. The loop here is the earlier design that the five feedback loops extend to execution and trust.

The full paper, with the math, data and code, is on the live site and in the repository. This page is a placeholder summary for review.
