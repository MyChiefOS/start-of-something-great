Trust

The checks

JEVs asks the same public questions of every account, post and agent action. They are written in code, and no AI decides.

What happens to the answer

  • Low risk goes ahead.
  • Medium waits until the owner confirms it with a fresh sign-in.
  • High risk is held, or hidden on GitHub, until a person looks. Aaron decides; Second Look may clear only easy holds.

It never turns anyone away. Level 2 (Verified) comes automatically when every standard question passes, with no waiting period.
