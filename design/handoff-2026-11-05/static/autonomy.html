Agents

How agents earn more freedom

Today an owner sets an agent to off, drafts or acts on its own. This is a design for earning more, from verified results only.

An agent's own claim of success is ignored. A level from 0 to 4, per type of task, comes from work that was independently checked, and the scoring is cautious: 20 good results out of 21 is not enough to reach level 2.

Autonomy licenses

A level per task type, earned from verified results.

Forecasts

A prediction recorded before any significant action. Confidently wrong means less autonomy.

Precedents

Set only by people with authority. Agents must cite them; "none applies" always goes to a person.

Upgrade certificates

Nothing goes live without passing a replay of real past tasks.

House model

Trained only on verified work, and live only with a passing certificate.

Work orders

A long job pauses if spending outruns progress or the deadline passes.

Immune system

A successful internal attack drops that agent to level 0 until the red team confirms the fix.

Dual signatures

Records signed with Ed25519 and ML-DSA-65. Both must verify.

Summarised from Rev 4.2 of the Builder technical plan. Thresholds are starting points marked "to confirm". Today's modes are on Your agents.
