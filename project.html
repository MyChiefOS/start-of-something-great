Schedule

Planned against verified work

The plan is the points it has due by each day. Verified is work with an anchored receipt. The gap between them is how far ahead or behind the project is. A task joins the first milestone due on or after the day it was posted, so the plan fills itself as tasks arrive.

    How it’s figured

    The schedule index is verified points divided by planned points. Under 1.00 is behind, over 1.00 is ahead. The forecast assumes the last seven days’ pace holds. Points are signs of audited work, not money.
